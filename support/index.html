Help & Feedback

For app feedback, open Settings → Help & Feedback → Send Feedback. Lockerly opens your email app and includes the app and iOS version.

Clean stickers

Photograph one item against a clean, contrasting background. Review the cutout, name, and category before saving.

Reminders

Open an item, enable Set reminder, choose a future date, and save. Notification permission must be allowed in iPhone Settings.

iCloud backup

Enable iCloud backup in Settings, choose whether to include original photos, then use Back Up Now. Restore replaces the local inventory on that device.
