Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

Inventory data

Items, spaces, notes, and settings are stored on your device. Lockerly does not require an account for local use.

物品、空间、备注和设置保存在你的设备上。本地使用 Lockerly 无需注册账户。

Photos and recognition

Photos you choose are used to create item stickers. When cloud recognition is used, the selected image may be sent to the configured recognition service to return a suggested name and category.

你选择的照片用于制作物品贴纸。使用云端识别时，所选图片可能会发送到已配置的识别服务。

iCloud backup

If you enable iCloud backup, Lockerly stores a backup in your private Apple CloudKit database. Apple manages the iCloud account and infrastructure.

开启 iCloud 备份后，Lockerly 会将备份存储在你的 Apple CloudKit 私有数据库中。

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device after you grant permission. You can disable them in Lockerly or iPhone Settings.

获得授权后，物品提醒会在设备上排期。你可在 Lockerly 或 iPhone 设置中关闭通知。

Deletion and retention

Deleting local data removes it from this device. An existing iCloud backup remains until replaced or removed through the app or your iCloud data controls.

删除本地数据会将其从此设备移除。已有的 iCloud 备份会保留，直到被替换或删除。
