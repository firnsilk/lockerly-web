Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

Lockerly helps you keep track of what you own. Your inventory is stored on your device unless you choose iCloud backup. This policy explains the limited data processing used for item recognition and optional features.

Lockerly 帮助你记录和管理自己的物品。除非你选择使用 iCloud 备份，物品数据会保存在你的设备上。本政策说明物品识别及可选功能所需的有限数据处理。

Information we collect

Information you provide voluntarily. Lockerly does not require an account or registration for local use. If you contact us by email, we receive the address you send from and use it only to reply.

你自愿提供的信息。本地使用 Lockerly 无需注册账号。如果你通过邮件联系我们，我们会收到你的发信地址，仅用于回复。

Information collected automatically. Lockerly does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking. When cloud recognition is requested, Apple App Attest supplies an app-install integrity proof to protect the service from abuse.

自动处理的信息。Lockerly 不使用分析服务、广告 SDK 或跨 App 追踪。发起云端识别时，Apple App Attest 会提供 App 安装完整性证明，用于保护识别服务、防止滥用。

Photos and item recognition. Lockerly extracts the photographed object on your device to create a sticker. After extraction succeeds, a compressed image of that object (up to 640 pixels on its longest side) is sent for one cloud recognition request to suggest its name and category. You may cancel recognition and enter these details yourself. Lockerly does not retain the recognition image after the short processing window.

照片与物品识别。Lockerly 会先在设备上抠出拍摄的物体，用于制作贴纸。抠图成功后，该物体的压缩图片（最长边不超过 640 像素）会发起一次云端识别，用于建议名称和分类。你可以取消识别并自行填写。Lockerly 不会在短暂处理窗口结束后保留识别图片。

Data storage and backup

Your items, spaces, notes, and settings are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud backup, Lockerly stores a backup in your private Apple CloudKit database; Apple manages the iCloud account, encryption, and infrastructure, and Lockerly cannot access it.

你的物品、空间、备注和设置保存在设备本地。开启 iCloud 备份后，Lockerly 会将备份存储在你私有的 Apple CloudKit 数据库中；iCloud 账户、加密和基础设施均由 Apple 管理，Lockerly 无法访问这些数据。

Third-party services

Cloud vision recognition. The processed object image and your category names are sent over an encrypted connection through Tencent Cloud SCF (mainland China first) or Cloudflare Workers (other regions first) to Zhipu AI GLM. If a network connection fails, Lockerly may try the other route. The data is used to return one suggested name and category. App Attest integrity proofs are used only for service security, not advertising or tracking.

云端识图。处理后的物体图片和你的分类名称会通过加密连接，经腾讯云 SCF（中国大陆优先）或 Cloudflare Workers（其他地区优先）发送至智谱 AI GLM。如网络连接失败，Lockerly 可能尝试另一条路由。这些数据用于返回一次建议名称和分类。App Attest 完整性证明仅用于服务安全，不用于广告或追踪。

Apple. iCloud backup uses Apple CloudKit, and subscriptions are processed through the Apple App Store. Apple manages payments, subscription billing, and iCloud infrastructure under Apple's own privacy terms.

Apple。iCloud 备份使用 Apple CloudKit，订阅通过 Apple App Store 处理。支付、订阅计费和 iCloud 基础设施均由 Apple 按照其自身隐私条款管理。

Subscriptions

Lockerly Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through the Apple App Store. Lockerly does not receive or store your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Lockerly Pro 是通过 Apple App Store 销售的自动续订订阅。Lockerly 不会获取或存储你的支付信息。你可以在自己的 Apple ID 设置中管理或取消订阅。

Analytics and advertising

Lockerly does not integrate any analytics or advertising SDKs, does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Lockerly 未集成任何分析或广告 SDK，不使用 Cookie 或类似追踪技术，也不会跨应用或跨网站追踪你。

Notifications

Lockerly uses local notifications: when you set an item reminder or enable the daily summary, the app schedules the notification on your device and iOS delivers it at the scheduled time. Notification content is generated on your device — Lockerly does not use remote (push) notifications and does not send your reminders or notification content anywhere. You can disable notifications in Lockerly or iPhone Settings at any time.

Lockerly 使用本地通知：当你设置物品提醒或开启每日摘要时，应用会在你的设备上排期通知，由 iOS 在预定时间送达。通知内容在你的设备上本地生成 —— Lockerly 不使用远程（推送）通知，也不会把你的提醒或通知内容发送到任何地方。你随时可以在 Lockerly 或 iPhone 设置中关闭通知。

Deletion and retention

Deleting local data removes it from this device. An existing iCloud backup remains until it is replaced or removed through the app or your iCloud data controls. Because Lockerly does not retain personal data on its own servers, there is nothing further to erase.

删除本地数据会将其从此设备移除。已有的 iCloud 备份会保留，直到被替换或通过应用及 iCloud 数据管理功能删除。由于 Lockerly 不在自己的服务器上保留个人数据，因此没有需要进一步清除的内容。

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device and Lockerly keeps no personal data of its own, rights such as access, correction, and erasure are exercised directly in the app by editing or deleting your inventory. If you believe you have sent us personal data and want it removed, contact us and we will help.

由于你的数据保存在自己的设备上，且 Lockerly 自身不保留任何个人数据，访问、更正、删除等权利可直接在应用内通过编辑或删除你的物品数据来实现。如果你认为曾向我们发送过个人数据并希望删除，请联系我们，我们将协助处理。

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date at the top.

如果本政策发生变化，更新版本将发布在本页面，并在顶部标注新的生效日期。

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, email us at lockerlyapp@outlook.com.

如对本政策或你的数据有疑问，请发送邮件至 lockerlyapp@outlook.com。
